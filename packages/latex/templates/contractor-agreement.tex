\begin{document}
\maketitle
\jprow{Company}{\textbf{Example Pty Ltd} (ABN 00 000 000 000)}
\jprow{Contractor}{\textbf{Alex Example} (ABN 22 222 222 222)}
\jprow{Effective}{\jpmono\small 12 November 2026}
\jprow{Fee}{\jpmono\small \$95 per hour, excluding GST}
\jprowend

\section{The work}
\clause The Contractor will provide network design and installation services as agreed with the Company from time to time.
\clause The Contractor decides how and when the work is done, within the dates agreed for each task.

\section{Independent contractor}
\clause The Contractor is not an employee of the Company. This agreement does not create a partnership or agency.
\clause The Contractor is responsible for their own tax, superannuation, insurance and equipment.

\section{Payment}
\clause The Contractor will invoice monthly. The Company will pay within 14 days of a correct invoice.

\section{Confidentiality and intellectual property}
\clause The Contractor must keep the Company's information confidential during and after the agreement.
\clause The Company owns the work the Contractor makes for it under this agreement.

\section{Insurance}
\clause The Contractor will hold public liability insurance of at least \$5 million and give the Company a certificate on request.

\section{Ending the agreement}
\clause Either party may end this agreement with 14 days' written notice.
\clause The Company will pay for work done up to the end date.
