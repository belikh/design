\section{The client}
Bright Smiles is a four-chair dental practice in Brisbane with eleven staff. Patient records and imaging live on the network, so downtime means cancelled appointments.

\section{The challenge}
Imaging files took minutes to load and the phones dropped out several times a week. The old router was seven years old and nobody knew what was connected to it.

\section{What we did}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Mapped every device and removed what was not needed.
  \item Replaced the router and switch, and added two access points.
  \item Put phones, imaging and guests on separate networks.
  \item Added monitoring with an alert to our support line.
\end{itemize}

\section{The result}
In the 90 days since the install there have been no dropped calls and no unplanned downtime. Large images open in seconds.
