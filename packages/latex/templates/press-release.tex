\vspace{5mm}
\noindent\textbf{Brisbane, 12 November 2026.} Example Pty Ltd today opened an office in Sydney, its first outside Queensland. The team of six will support the company's existing clients in the state and take on new ones from January.

``Our clients asked us to be closer,'' said Alex Example, managing director. ``A local team means faster visits and people who know the buildings.''

The new office is in the CBD and opens on 1 December. Example Pty Ltd now supports more than 140 offices across Australia.
