\section{Definitions}
\clause \term{Confidential Information} means any information that one party (the \term{Discloser}) gives the other (the \term{Recipient}) in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.
\clause \term{Purpose} means evaluating and, if agreed, carrying out a commercial relationship between the parties.

\section{Obligations}
\clause The Recipient must keep the Confidential Information secret and use it only for the Purpose.
\clause The Recipient may share it only with people who need it for the Purpose and who are bound to keep it confidential.
\clause The Recipient must protect it with at least the care it gives its own confidential information.

\section{Exceptions}
\clause This agreement does not apply to information that is public through no fault of the Recipient, was already known to the Recipient, or was developed independently.
\clause The Recipient may disclose information where the law requires it, after telling the Discloser where it can.

\section{Term}
\clause This agreement lasts for two years from the effective date. The obligations in clause 02 continue for three years after it ends.

\section{Return of information}
\clause On request the Recipient must return or destroy the Confidential Information and confirm in writing that it has done so.

\section{General}
\clause This agreement is governed by the law of Queensland. The parties submit to the courts of that State.
\clause This agreement is the whole agreement on its subject and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.
