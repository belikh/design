\section{Purpose}
This procedure restores a client's files from the nightly backup after data loss. It applies to all managed clients.

\section{Scope}
Applies to the support team. It does not cover restoring a full server, which is SOP-021.

\section{Roles}
\begin{jpwide}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{50mm}p{86mm}@{}}
\toprule
\jplabel{Role} & \jplabel{Responsibility}\\
\midrule
Support engineer & Carries out the restore and records it\\
Team lead & Approves restores of more than 50 GB\\
Client contact & Confirms what is needed and checks the result\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{jpwide}

\section{Procedure}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Confirm the client's identity and what needs restoring.
  \item Open the backup console and select the latest good backup.
  \item Restore to a new folder, never over the original.
  \item Ask the client contact to check the files.
  \item Move the files into place once confirmed.
  \item Record the restore in the ticket.
\end{enumerate}
\begin{jpnote}[warn]Never restore over live files before the client has checked the copy.\end{jpnote}

\section{Records}
Each restore is logged in the ticket with the backup date, the files restored and who confirmed the result.

\section{Review}
This procedure is reviewed every year by the team lead.
