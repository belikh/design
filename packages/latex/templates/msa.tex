\agreementref{MSA-2026-003}
\title{Master services agreement}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
\jprow{Supplier}{\textbf{Example Pty Ltd} (ABN 00 000 000 000)}
\jprow{Client}{\textbf{Client Pty Ltd} (ABN 11 111 111 111)}
\jprow{Effective}{\jpmono\small 12 November 2026}
\jprowend

\section{Services}
\clause The Supplier will provide the services described in each statement of work signed by both parties.
\clause Each statement of work forms part of this agreement. If they conflict, the statement of work applies to that work only.

\section{Fees and payment}
\clause The Client will pay the fees in the statement of work. Invoices are due within 14 days.
\clause Late amounts carry interest at 2 percent above the Reserve Bank cash rate.
\clause Fees do not include GST, which the Client will pay in addition.

\section{Client responsibilities}
\clause The Client will give the Supplier the access, information and decisions it needs, in good time.

\section{Intellectual property}
\clause Each party keeps the intellectual property it brought to the work.
\clause On payment in full, the Client owns the deliverables made for it, except for the Supplier's tools and methods, which the Client may use as part of them.

\section{Confidentiality}
\clause Each party must keep the other's confidential information secret and use it only for this agreement.

\section{Liability}
\clause Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.
\clause Neither party is liable for lost profit or indirect loss.

\section{Term and termination}
\clause This agreement continues until either party ends it with 30 days' written notice.
\clause Either party may end it at once if the other breaches it and does not fix the breach within 14 days of notice.

\section{General}
\clause This agreement is governed by the law of Queensland.
\clause Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.

\jpsign{Example Pty Ltd}{Client Pty Ltd}
\end{document}
