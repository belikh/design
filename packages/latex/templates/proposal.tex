\section{Summary}
Your office network drops connections in the meeting rooms and slows down in the afternoon. We propose to replace the access points, separate guest and office traffic, and monitor the result for three months.

\section{The problem}
The current network was installed in 2018 for 12 people. It now serves 34 staff and many more devices.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Wi-Fi drops in two meeting rooms every day.
  \item Guests and staff share one network.
  \item Nobody is told when something fails.
\end{itemize}

\section{Our approach}
\subsection{Survey}
We measure signal and traffic across the floor over two days.
\subsection{Build}
We install new access points and a switch out of hours, then move devices across in stages.
\subsection{Monitor}
We watch the network for three months and fix what shows up.
