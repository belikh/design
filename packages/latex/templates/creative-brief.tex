\section{Background}
Example Pty Ltd is launching a new range in spring. Awareness is low outside the existing customer base.

\section{Objective}
Introduce the range to new customers and lift sign-ups to the mailing list by 20 percent in eight weeks.

\section{Audience}
Homeowners aged 30 to 55 who research purchases online and trust recommendations from people they know.

\section{Message}
\jpentry{Main}{One idea}
Quiet, well-made things that last.

\jpentry{Support}{Three points}
Built locally. Repairable. Backed by a ten-year guarantee.

\section{Deliverables}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Campaign concept and visual direction
  \item Six social posts and two short videos
  \item Landing page design
  \item Email template
\end{itemize}

\section{Tone and references}
Warm and plain. No slang, no hype. References: the 2025 catalogue and the brand guidelines.
