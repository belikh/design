\section{The role}
You look after the networks of around forty small offices, answer their questions and fix what breaks. You work with a buddy for the first month and take on your own clients from the second.

\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Answer support requests within the agreed response times.
  \item Monitor client networks and act on alerts.
  \item Document each change and each fix.
  \item Take part in the on-call roster, one week in six.
\end{itemize}

\section{Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Two or more years in IT support or network administration.
  \item Working knowledge of VLANs, DHCP, DNS and Wi-Fi.
  \item Clear written communication.
\end{itemize}

\section{Helpful but not required}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A networking certification
  \item Experience with UniFi or similar equipment
\end{itemize}
