# D059 geometry: sources and build

`au-states.json` and `au-lga.json` are generated by `build-au.mjs` (run once, output committed; the app never fetches at runtime and loads them with a dynamic import).

| File | Source | Licence | Retrieved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `au-states.json` | Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 1 - States, Provinces, `ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces` (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson) | Public domain (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) | 2026-11-13 |
| `au-lga.json` | ABS ASGS Edition 3, Local Government Areas 2021, ArcGIS REST service https://geo.abs.gov.au/arcgis/rest/services/ASGS2021/LGA/MapServer/0 | CC BY 4.0, (c) Australian Bureau of Statistics 2021 (https://www.abs.gov.au/website-privacy-copyright-and-disclaimer) | 2026-11-13 |

Coverage: all 8 states and territories and about 544 local government areas across Australia (the ABS "no usual address", "migratory" and "other territories" pseudo-areas, Jervis Bay, Lord Howe and Macquarie Islands are omitted).

Simplification: Douglas-Peucker at 0.02 degree (states, about 2 km) and 0.02 degree on top of ABS's own 0.01 degree server-side generalisation (LGAs); small islands dropped; coordinates quantised to 0.01 degree and delta-encoded. Boundaries are for display and coarse analysis, not legal use. Tiny LGAs (a few km2) that vanish at this scale are kept as boxes; holes (enclaves) are not represented. Neighbouring LGAs are simplified independently, so hairline gaps of up to a pixel can appear at high zoom.

Rebuild: `node build-au.mjs [cacheDir]` (needs network the first time; the cache holds the 40 MB Natural Earth file).
